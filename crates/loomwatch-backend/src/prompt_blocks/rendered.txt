===== tool: read_file
Read a file. Text comes with its lines numbered, and so does the text of a PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or PowerPoint (.pptx) file, and an email (.eml) as who wrote to whom, when, its subject and its words. For a long file, read it in parts with offset and limit. A picture (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP) is shown to you when the model you run on can see pictures.

  "additionalProperties": false,
  "properties": {
    "limit": {
      "description": "How many lines to read. Default: 2000.",
      "minimum": 1,
      "type": "integer"
    },

      "description": "The first line to read, counting from 1.",
      "minimum": 1,
      "type": "integer"
