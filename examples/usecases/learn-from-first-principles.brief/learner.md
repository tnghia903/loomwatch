# About the learner

Every agent on the team reads this before it starts. Change it to fit whoever is learning.

- **Start from:** what a curious five-year-old knows from everyday life. Assume no school science,
  no maths beyond counting, and no technical words.
- **I like pictures from:** the kitchen, the playground, games and trips.
- **One lesson should take:** about ten minutes to read.
- **Language:** English.
