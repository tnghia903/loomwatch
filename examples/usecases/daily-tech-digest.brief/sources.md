# Trusted sources and what I care about

Every agent on the team reads this before it starts. Edit it to change where the news comes from
and what makes the cut.

## Primary sources: the thing itself

- The company's own newsroom, blog, release notes or changelog (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and
  DeepMind, Apple Newsroom, Microsoft, Meta, NVIDIA, AMD, GitHub Changelog, AWS)
- Papers on arXiv (cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG, cs.CR) and their authors' own pages
- Regulators and governments: the European Commission, the US FTC, the US Department of Commerce
  (BIS), the UK CMA, and Singapore's IMDA
- Company filings with the SEC

## Newsrooms

- Reuters Technology, the Associated Press, Bloomberg Technology, the Financial Times
- The Verge, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, Wired, MIT Technology Review, IEEE Spectrum, 404 Media

## Pointers, never sources

Hacker News, Techmeme, Reddit and posts on social media can lead to a story. They never count as
its source: follow them to a primary source or a newsroom above.

## The rule for keeping a story

A story makes the digest only if its primary source says it, or at least two independent
newsrooms above report it. For a paywalled page, use only what is visible without paying.

## What I care about

- **Most:** AI models and tools, developer tools, chips and hardware, security incidents, and
  rules and regulation that change what can be built or sold.
- **Skip:** funding rounds under US$100 million, rumours, opinion pieces, stock price moves, and
  product deals.
- **Length:** five to eight stories, readable in five minutes.
