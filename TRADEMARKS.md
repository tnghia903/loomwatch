# The LoomWatch name, logo and crew

The code of LoomWatch is free under the [MIT License](LICENSE-MIT) or the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE-APACHE). Those licenses cover the code. They don't cover the
LoomWatch name, its logo or its crew figures, which identify this project. The Apache License says
so in section 6. This page says what you can do with them.

## What this covers

- **The name** LoomWatch.
- **The logo:** the crew's face sewn onto its tile, the wordmark and the two together. The masters
  are in [docs/assets/brand](docs/assets/brand). Copies are the app icons in `packaging/macos/`,
  `ui/public/` and `site/`.
- **The crew figures:** the sewing-box characters the agents wear, their clothes and their
  workbench. They are in `ui/public/agent/` and `site/assets/crew/`.

The logo and figure files are not under the MIT or Apache License. Copyright © 2026 Nghia Thai,
all rights reserved, except for the uses below.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the name to talk about LoomWatch: write about it, review it, teach it, link to it.
- Say that your project works with LoomWatch, or is based on it.
- Share copies of LoomWatch as it is, from the releases or built from this repository, with the
  logo and the figures inside.
- Show screenshots and videos of LoomWatch.
- Change the code and run your changed copy yourself, or inside your company.

## What needs permission

- Calling your fork, product or service LoomWatch, or anything people could mistake for it.
- Using the logo or the crew figures in your own product, service, website or marketing.
- Offering LoomWatch to others as a hosted or paid service under its name.
- Suggesting that LoomWatch, or its author, made, endorses or supports your work.

## If you share a changed version

You are welcome to, under the code license. Give it a name of its own, and replace the logo and the
crew figures. You can say that it is based on LoomWatch.

## Asking

To ask for permission, start a [discussion](https://github.com/tnghia903/loomwatch/discussions).
