      What it does
      
          Everything LoomWatch does, in the order you use it.
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Step 1
          Agents
          
        
            
          
          • Build an agent once newIts job, app, model and what it may do, as plain blocks.
            • 
          
          • A face you pick newSix figures, seven colours, the same everywhere.
            • 
          
          • Try it on its ownA real request on the real app, before a team relies on it.
            • 
          
          • VersionsTeams keep theirs until you choose Update.
            • 
          
          • Asks before it actsWeb, files and commands wait for your OK, unless you allow them.
            • 
          
          • Skills when neededFrom your computer, opened only when a step calls for one.
            • 
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Step 2
          Workflow
          
        
            
          
          • Start from your agents newSaved agents are one click away in every team.
            • 
          
          • Hire by job, or describe itDescribe it in plain words; LoomWatch proposes the team, and you press Apply.
            • 
          
          • Stops where you decidePut yourself in the workflow as a review step.
            • 
          
          • Give each agent what it needsFolders, files, Notion pages, the team's notebook.
            • 
          
          • Make it work betterSuggestions from its last five runs, one click to apply, and about how many tokens a run uses.
            • 
          
          • On a scheduleRuns while you're away, if your Mac is awake and LoomWatch is open; anything that would ask is declined.
            • 
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Step 3
          Chat
          
        
            
          
          • One chat per team@team starts it, @Writer asks one agent.
            • 
          
          • Status in plain words new“Writing its reply”, “Waiting for you”, “Using a tool”.
            • 
          
          • Talk while it worksNotes reach the agent at work; Send now interrupts.
            • 
          
          • Approve, or send it backYour note travels with the work.
            • 
          
          • Answers with receiptsFiles, sources and every step, on a timeline you can replay.
            • 
          
          • Never miss a questionOne tray for all your teams; answers can go to Notion.
            • 
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      Underneath
      
            
        
          • On your computerYour files and history stay on your Mac. No account, no cloud of its own.
            • 
        
          • The AI you already haveClaude Code, Codex and OpenCode, mixed on one team.
            • 
        
          • Every step recordedCalls, handovers and answers, kept so you can check them.
            • 
        
          • Free and open sourceOne command installs it; Update keeps it current.
            • 
      
          
    
          
    
          To be exact about privacy: LoomWatch itself sends nothing anywhere unless you turn on Send to Notion. Each agent's own app still sends prompts and files to its model provider, as it does when you use it directly.
          
  
          

          



          
  
          
    
          
      
          
        Start
        
          One command, and your first team is already waiting.
          
        
          Open Terminal on your Mac (it's in Applications → Utilities), paste this, and press Return. It downloads a ready-built LoomWatch, checks it, and opens it in your browser with a demo team that works offline, so you can try it before you connect any AI app. Nothing to compile.
          
        
          
          
          A Mac
          macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. Linux builds exist but are untested, and Windows isn't supported yet.
          
          
          Docker Desktop or OrbStack
          An app that runs the database for your team chats and their history. Install one first; if it's missing, LoomWatch tells you.
          
          
          An AI app for Terminal
          For real runs: Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode. OpenCode has free models and needs no account. The demo team needs none.
          
          
          No GitHub account needed
          The installer fetches the public release, so anyone can install it.
          
        
          
        
          Read the installer before you run it, or build from source.
          
      
          
      
          
        
          Terminal
