Free and open source · runs on Mac and Windows · no code needed

Put AI agents to work as one team. They improve themselves.

A team of AI agents on your Mac or Windows PC, each thinking with the model you pick: Claude, GPT, Gemini or a free one, on the ChatGPT or Claude plan you already have, or one OpenRouter key. They pass work to each other, pause for your OK wherever you choose, keep a record of every step, and learn from a checker and from you.

      Step 2Workflow

      Put your agents in a team. Check the plan in one sentence.

      Connect them in order, and put yourself wherever you want to check the work. LoomWatch reads the whole team back as one plain sentence before anything runs. Then drag in the folders, files and skills each agent needs.

      Or just describe it
      Describe the job, and Ask LoomWatch proposes a team on the canvas. Nothing changes until you press Apply.

      Step 3Chat

      ThenIt improves
      
          Each agent improves itself. You see what it learned.
          
      
          When a checker in the team sends work back and the fix passes, the agent can keep a one-line lesson. What you say about its answers, and answers you love, teach it too. Its lessons are kept in My agents and go with it to every team.
          
      
          
        
          Only real feedback teaches it
          Work a checker sent back, what you say about an answer, or an answer you love. It doesn't grade itself.
          
        
          Take a lesson back
          Each one says where it came from. Undo it in the chat, or Forget it on the agent.

      What it does
      
          Everything LoomWatch does, in the order you use it.
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Step 1
          Agents
          
        
            
          
          • Build an agent onceEvery team uses it as you last saved it.
            • 
          
          • A face you pickSix figures, seven colours.
            • 
          
          • Makes real filesSlides, Word, Excel, charts and dashboards. PDFs and videos need a program it names.
            • 
          
          • Try it on its ownA real run before a team relies on it.
            • 
          
          • Asks before it actsWeb, files and commands wait for your OK.
            • 
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Step 2
          Workflow
          
        
            
          
          • Hire, or describe itPick saved agents, or describe the job.
            • 
          
          • Stops where you decideYou review before the work goes on.
            • 
          
          • Give each agent what it needsFolders, files, skills and Notion pages.
            • 
          
          • On a scheduleRuns by itself while LoomWatch is open.
            • 
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Step 3
          Chat
          
        
            
          
          • One chat per team@team starts it, @Writer asks one agent.
            • 
          
          • Talk while it worksIt says what it's doing; your notes reach it.
            • 
          
          • Approve, or send it backYour note travels with the work.
            • 
          
          • Answers with receiptsFiles, sources and every step it took.
            • 
          
          • Ask about any partWords, pictures, cells, slides, pages or a moment in a video.
            • 
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Then
          It improves
          
        
            
          
          • Learns from a checkerWork a checker sends back, once fixed, can become a lesson.
            • 
          
          • Learns from youWhat you say about its answers, and answers you love.
            • 
          
          • Takes lessons to every teamIt's the same agent in each one.
            • 
          
          • Undo any lessonEach one says where it came from.
            • 
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      Underneath
      
            
        
          • On your computerNo account, no cloud of its own.
            • 
        
          • Your plan, or one keyChatGPT, Claude, or any model on OpenRouter.
            • 
        
          • Every step recordedCalls, handovers, answers.
            • 
        
          • Free and open sourceUpdates in one click.
            • 
      
          
    
          
    
          Your teams and their history stay on your computer. Agents send their work to the company whose model you picked (OpenAI, Anthropic, or through OpenRouter), and nothing else leaves unless you turn on Send to Notion.
