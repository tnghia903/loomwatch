Get started with LoomWatch. Your first answer in a few minutes.

LoomWatch is a free app that puts a few AI helpers, called agents, to work on one job together, and shows you every step. This guide installs it and gets you a first answer from an agent. No code, and no LoomWatch account.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 11 or newer (Apple silicon or Intel), or a Windows PC with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
  • Nothing else to install first, and you don't need to know how to code.
  • One way to pay for AI models: your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, your Claude Max or Team plan's API credit, or an OpenRouter key. LoomWatch asks which when you send your first request.

Install on a Mac

  1. Download LoomWatch-macos.dmg (about 30 MB) and open it.
  2. Drag LoomWatch into Applications.
  3. Open LoomWatch from Applications.
Download for Mac macOS 11 or newer

If macOS won't open it: LoomWatch isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS may say it can't check it for harmful software. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway beside LoomWatch. macOS asks only once.

LoomWatch opens in your web browser. It then waits in a black notch at the top middle of each screen. If your MacBook has a notch around its camera, that’s the one; on any other screen, LoomWatch draws one in the middle of the menu bar. Point at it to see what your teams are doing, and click ⋯ there for LoomWatch’s menu. There is no window to keep open: closing the browser tab leaves LoomWatch running until you choose Quit LoomWatch in that menu.

To message one of your agents from any app, press ⌃⌥Space (Control-Option-Space), or choose Message and its name at the top of LoomWatch’s menu. A box opens at the top middle of the screen you are on; Tab switches to another of your agents, and its figure opens its workbench. When you send, the agent shows at its workbench for a moment, then waits at the top of the screen while it works: point at it to watch. Its answer drops down there: the copy button at its top right keeps its headings, lists and links when you paste, and Open in LoomWatch shows it whole. To hand it a file, a picture, a link or some text, drag it to the top middle of the screen. The box opens with the agent you last wrote to. If that agent asks one of your teams for help, the team starts only when you press Start, and that run costs what any run of the team costs. To hide the notch and all of this, turn off Show at the notch in the menu. LoomWatch’s face then sits at the right of the menu bar: click it for the same menu, where you can turn the notch back on.

Install on Windows

  1. Download LoomWatch-windows-setup.exe and open it.
  2. It installs LoomWatch for you alone (no administrator needed), adds it to the Start menu and opens it.
Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

If Windows says it protected your PC: LoomWatch isn't signed with a code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

LoomWatch opens in your web browser and keeps an icon beside the clock (click the ^ there if you can't see it). Click the icon to open LoomWatch again.

Your first run

The first time LoomWatch opens, your first agent asks What shall we make first?

  1. Type what you want made, such as Plan a 30-minute lesson about animals, or pick one of the examples under the box, and press Enter.
  2. If no AI is connected yet, LoomWatch asks which one should answer: your ChatGPT plan, your Claude plan's API credit or an OpenRouter key, each with what it costs. Connect one, and your request goes on.
  3. Read the answer. Then click Tell my agents about me: your name, what you do, the language you want and how long. Click Answer that again, and your agent answers the same request again, made for you.
  4. Click Keep this agent to keep it in My agents, or Go to Home.

Rather look around first? Click Just look around at the top. You can change what your agents know about you any time in About you, in the ☰ menu.
